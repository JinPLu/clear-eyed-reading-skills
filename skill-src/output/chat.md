# Chat Output

Follow the user's language. Use readable Markdown and natural headings without exposing the internal reading process as a checklist. Keep citations close to the facts or judgments they support.

When a diagram is needed, prefer one concise Mermaid flowchart or relationship diagram. Keep it simple and explanatory; do not build custom HTML, interactive artifacts, or a diagram suite for an ordinary reading. If Mermaid is unavailable, use a compact plain-text flow that is readable exactly as displayed.
